Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

mergers.news ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

mergers.news is designed to be minimally invasive. We collect very limited data:

Server LogsIP address, browser type, pages visited, timestampsSecurity monitoring, error diagnosis

We do not collect, store, or process: names, email addresses, payment information, location data, or any personally identifiable information (PII) through normal use of the Site.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics pixels
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks
  • We do not build user profiles or track individual behaviour across sessions
  • We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking tools

3. Third-Party Services

mergers.news uses the following third-party services which may process limited technical data:

  • Vercel — Website hosting and deployment. Subject to Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub — Deal database storage (public repository). Subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts — Font delivery. May log requests per Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic API — Used server-side only for automated nightly deal updates. No user data is sent to Anthropic.

4. Cookies

mergers.news does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. The site may use browser-level caching (Service Worker) for performance, which stores static assets locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is used solely to improve loading speed.

5. External Links

When you click a "View Original Source" link, you will be directed to a third-party website (e.g. Reuters, Bloomberg, PRNewswire, company investor relations pages). These sites have their own privacy policies which we do not control. We recommend reviewing their policies before interacting with them.

6. Data Retention

Server logs maintained by our hosting provider (Vercel) are retained according to their standard data retention policies. We do not independently retain personal data.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including:

  • GDPR (EU/EEA users): Right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection
  • CCPA (California users): Right to know, delete, and opt-out of sale (we do not sell data)
  • UK GDPR: Same rights as EU GDPR apply

Because we collect minimal personal data through normal Site use, most requests will confirm that we hold no personal data about you. To exercise any rights, contact: contact@mergers.news

8. Children's Privacy

mergers.news is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Security

We implement technical security measures including HTTPS encryption, strict Content Security Policy headers, HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), and X-Frame-Options to protect the Site from common web vulnerabilities. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of the Site following any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
