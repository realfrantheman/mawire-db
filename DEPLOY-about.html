What We Do

mergers.news aggregates corporate transaction data directly from the SEC's EDGAR system — the authoritative source for US public company M&A disclosures. Every deal on this platform originates from an official regulatory filing. We extract deal terms, acquirer and target identities, transaction values, and premiums from the filing documents themselves.

We present this data in a format designed for professionals who need deal intelligence without subscribing to expensive terminal services. All data originates from publicly available SEC filings. We do not publish material non-public information (MNPI).

SEC Filing Types We Track
FormFiled ByWhat It Covers
DEFM14ATarget companyDefinitive merger proxy — seeks shareholder approval for a proposed merger. Contains full agreement, fairness opinion, and per-share consideration.
SC TO-TAcquirerTender offer statement — formal offer to acquire all outstanding shares of a public company at a specified price per share.
S-4AcquirerMerger registration — registers shares to be issued as stock consideration in a merger. Includes the full merger agreement and pro forma financials.
SC 13E-3Issuer / AcquirerGoing-private transaction — LBOs, management buyouts, and sponsor-led take-privates requiring the company to be delisted from public markets.
